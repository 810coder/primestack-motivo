Meet Seyi

Arc. Oluwaseyi Oyebode

Seyi holds both a B.Tech and M.Tech degree from the Federal University of Technology, Akure, which he completed in 2013 and 2014, respectively. With over a decade of experience, he has established himself as a skilled architectural designer, contributing to the design and execution of a wide range of projects. His portfolio includes residential buildings, educational facilities, civic centers, car parks, recreational spaces, and urban renewal projects. Seyi has also taken on supervisory roles in several of these projects, ensuring quality and precision from concept to completion.
